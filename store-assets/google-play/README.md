# Google Play listing assets

- `app-icon-512.png`: 512 × 512 PNG app icon.
- `feature-graphic.png`: 1024 × 500 PNG feature graphic.
- `phone/`: four 1080 × 2160 screenshots captured from the current app UI in dark mode.
- `tablet-7-inch/`: four 1080 × 1921 portrait screenshots captured at a compact 7-inch tablet viewport in dark mode.
- `tablet-10-inch/`: four 1920 × 1080 screenshots captured at a 10-inch tablet viewport in dark mode.

Screenshots use synthetic review fixture data, not a real account. Refresh them after meaningful UI changes; the capture script is `scripts/capture-play-store-assets.mjs`.
