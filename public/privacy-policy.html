Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This policy explains how Pitaka handles information when you use the Pitaka website and personal finance app. Pitaka provides tools for tracking budgets, accounts, transactions, savings, recurring items, and investments. The app does not connect to your bank or move money.

Information we collect

  • Account information: email address, user ID, and, when provided by your sign-in provider, your display name and profile information. You can sign in with email and password or Google.
  • Finance information you enter: account and wallet names and balances, income and expenses, categories, budgets, transfers, savings goals, recurring income and bills, investment records, and related dates, amounts, currencies, and notes.
  • Support messages: your name, email address, topic, subject, and message when you use the contact form.
  • Payment records: if you buy Pro through the website, PayMongo processes the payment. Pitaka receives checkout and payment status information associated with your account. Pitaka does not receive your full card details from PayMongo.
  • Usage and performance information: Firebase Analytics, Vercel Analytics, and Vercel Speed Insights may receive app or page usage, browser or device details, and performance information.
  • Information stored on your device: preferences, an offline cache, summaries, and a small local error log used by the app. Downloads and exports you create are saved or shared by your device.

How we use information

We use this information to provide and secure your account, store and sync your finance records, show the app’s features, process Pro purchases, respond to support requests, maintain and improve the app, diagnose problems, and meet legal obligations.

Service providers and sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share information with service providers only as needed to operate Pitaka:

  • Google Firebase provides sign-in, cloud database, and analytics services.
  • Vercel hosts the website and provides website analytics and performance monitoring.
  • EmailJS processes contact form messages for delivery to Pitaka.
  • PayMongo processes website checkout payments and related payment records.

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. They may process information in countries where they or their service providers operate. We may also disclose information when required by law or when needed to protect users, the service, or our legal rights.

Storage and security

Account and finance records are stored with Firebase and associated with your account. Access controls are intended to restrict cloud records to the signed-in account that owns them. We use HTTPS for network connections and take reasonable steps to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Retention and deletion

We retain account and finance information while your account is active and as needed to provide the service, address support issues, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations. You can delete individual records in the app or request deletion of your account and its associated Pitaka records from the account deletion page or from More → Delete account & data in the app.

Account deletion removes your Pitaka sign-in and associated cloud records. It does not remove files you downloaded, copies on other devices, or records independently retained by payment or other service providers. Those providers apply their own retention policies and legal requirements.

Your choices

You can review, edit, or delete finance entries in the app, download an export, change device-local preferences, and request account deletion using the links above. You can also clear browser storage or uninstall the app to remove some device-local information; this does not delete your cloud account.

Children’s privacy

Pitaka is a personal finance tool and is not designed for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The latest version will be published at this address with its updated date.

Contact

Pitaka is the name of the app and service. For privacy questions or requests, contact the app publisher through the Pitaka contact form.
